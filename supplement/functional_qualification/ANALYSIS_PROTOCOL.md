# Functional qualification revision: fixed analysis protocol, 2 October 2026

Article: evidence-constrained engineering inverse design, not a population survey,
material experiment, predictive classifier or comparative LCA. Fixed source-model
flows, geography and scenario alternatives are inherited from v100. No EU grade
rate is estimated from experimental specimens. Analyses below are specified
before the new calculation script is run; literature exploration already occurred.

## Primary evidence and independent unit

Schönfeldt et al. (2025), DOI 10.1002/adem.202402815: six source-material
lineages, Tables 3–8; room-temperature Permagraph measurements, and three
device demonstrations at approximately 22 °C. Tables 5–7 supply the before/after
and source-composition values. Production waste is a process comparator, not an
additional postconsumer urban-mining stock. No individual raw measurements or
complete-route mass yields are published. The traction coercivity is censored
above 1950 kA/m; do not turn it into an exact value or a retention ratio.

Lagos et al. (2025), DOI 10.1051/metal/2025075: an independent elevator-motor
material lineage, three processing branches, Tables 1, 2 and 4. Finished-magnet
measurements are at 300 K. Table 4 calls the remanence Mr, in T; preserve this
name and distinguish it from powder measurements. These three branches are
not three independent source batches. Source composition is measured before
processing, not a verified final-composition measurement for every branch.

Use only published tabulations. The journal PDFs/HTML are open but unreported
underlying records are not. Values are reported point observations; no invented
replicate sizes, variances, confidence intervals, paired error covariance or yield.

## Route and endpoint typing

U: intact/component reuse, including cutting and recoating if magnet material is
not converted to powder. P: hydrogen-assisted powder recycling followed by new
magnet manufacture; optional composition correction must be recorded separately.
E: elemental recovery, with independently qualified purity and product yields.
No automatic nesting of U, P and E eligibility. A source's elemental content,
material response after a named process, geometry fit, device demonstration and
complete service qualification are different evidence levels. Unknown is not a
measured failure and does not justify acceptance. Direct reuse in Cherkezova et
al. (2025) is mechanically processed particles, not a demonstrated intact U route.

## Comparisons and primary endpoints

1. Fixed inherited chemistry gates: source Nd+Pr+Dy >=20% and >=30%.
   Total rare earths >=30% is a separate diagnostic, not an equivalent element gate.
2. Component-reference screens: all three tabulated output properties must meet
   each of the published primary-device triples in Schönfeldt Table 3. These
   triples are reference points, NOT minimum device specifications or certified
   acceptance rules. Compare chemical decisions against these point screens;
   use terms screen-false-accept/reject, not industrial misclassification rate.
3. Sensitivity: relax all three reference coordinates jointly by 0–15% (1%
   increments). This is a specification perturbation, not measurement uncertainty.
   Report all profiles and cases, not a selected favourable tolerance.
4. Retention ratios Br/post:pre, Hcj/post:pre and BHmax/post:pre for matched
   lineages. Censored Hcj ratios remain missing. Independent elevator material
   is an out-of-study counterexample to chemical sufficiency, not an independently
   validated trained predictor. Process branches are compared without fitting an
   oxygen/hydrogen threshold or treating powder yield as finished yield.
5. Actual device evidence: report the three published maximum-speed induced-EMF
   changes; compare against point screens, without assigning a fabricated torque,
   temperature, lifetime or corrosion pass label. A negative screen does not
   contradict a positive narrow bench endpoint. A positive screen is not proof of
   complete device function.

## Inverse-design link and identifiability

Keep 2030, k=2, R=750 km, whole-cap rule, union-eligibility ceiling 10%, wind
access 50%, source alternatives crossed, Nd+Pr+Dy minima 20/30%. Use inherited
same-node capped inputs. The 25% WEEE and 50% wind presumptive intact-compatible
shares remain illustrative envelopes, not calibrated grades. Introduce f_W and
f_Wi in [0,1], representing verified application qualification AND complete-route
mass retention within those envelopes. Compute the necessary intact-output cut
0.25 Q_W f_W + 0.50 Q_Wi f_Wi >=250 t/year. Its upper attainability does not prove
other route cuts, U service equivalence, actual deliveries or economic feasibility.
Its failure excludes the target within the declared envelope. Without population
qualification/yield data, the unconditional lower output bound is zero.

Finished functional output U and P, and separated-element output E, have different
reference flows. Do not sum them into a fabricated common functional unit. General
allocation uses measured/identified route-specific compatibility and output yields;
missing yields stay unknown. No primary displacement or net environmental benefit
is credited. The useful data product is the auditable source/process/endpoint map
and inverse qualification boundary, not a new network algorithm.
